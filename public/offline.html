No connection

This device can't reach the Fido server. Nothing can be saved to the system right now — but the shop can keep selling.

Selling while offline

  1. Sell only what is on the shelf in front of you. For a phone, write down the IMEI from the box.
  2. Take cash only. No credit sales, store credit, returns or reloads until the system is back.
  3. Write every sale below (or in the paper book) and give the customer a handwritten receipt. Tell them the printed invoice follows.
  4. When the connection returns, enter each sale at the POS in the same order, then tick it off here.

Sales written down on this device
